Start with the destination

Every guide follows the same discipline without pretending every image is interchangeable: identify the user and next tool, preserve the original, prepare visible input, test one representative result, measure the promised outcome, and record the limits. Use a guide that matches the job you must deliver rather than accepting whatever preview appears first.
